LA PULPERIA NICA — TONIGHT DEMO PLAN
Date: November 2, 2026

DEMO GOAL
Create a polished, clickable storefront prototype that communicates the La Pulperia Nica streetwear concept and its connection to Nicaraguan neighborhood culture. This is a presentation demo, not a live store.

CORE AUDIENCE
Nicaraguans in the United States who want clothing that helps them feel connected to home. Keep the store welcoming and easy to understand for other U.S. shoppers too.

TONIGHT'S SCOPE
- One responsive storefront landing page.
- A short hero message and a concise brand story.
- Three draft collection concepts, clearly presented as concepts.
- One product detail interaction with an illustrative price.
- A demo-only purchase button that displays a confirmation without collecting payment.
- A visible notice that products, prices, and checkout are not live.

SUGGESTED PAGE CONTENT
1. Hero: “Everyday clothing. Neighborhood soul.” Treat this as draft copy, not approved final brand language.
2. Collection: three concept cards with a title, short description, and “View design” interaction.
3. Product detail: concept description, illustrative price, and demo purchase button.
4. Our story: explain that the brand draws inspiration from the pulperia as a familiar neighborhood shop and social center.
5. Demo notice: “Prototype only — designs and prices are illustrative. No orders or payments are being taken.”

DESIGN GUIDANCE
Use a contemporary streetwear layout with a warm neighborhood-store character. Keep cultural references specific and respectful; use only references the founder can explain with confidence. Avoid generic souvenir styling and unsupported claims about cultural meaning. Existing project-folder images may be reviewed as inspiration, but do not assume they are approved La Pulperia assets.

TIMEBOX (ABOUT FOUR HOURS)
1. Pick a visual direction and three draft design ideas — 30 minutes.
2. Build the responsive landing page and collection cards — 90 minutes.
3. Add the product detail and demo-only purchase interaction — 45 minutes.
4. Refine mobile layout, copy, and the demo walkthrough — 45 minutes.
5. Keep 30 minutes as a buffer for setup and fixes.

TWO-HOUR FALLBACK
Build one polished landing page with a hero, three concept cards, a short story, and the demo notice. Skip the product detail interaction.

WALKTHROUGH (ABOUT TWO MINUTES)
1. Introduce the audience and the cultural connection behind the concept.
2. Show the storefront and explain the visual direction.
3. Open one concept and explain its intended meaning.
4. State what remains undecided before a real launch: approved designs, language, ecommerce platform, print-on-demand provider, budget, pricing, and launch timing.

DONE WHEN
- The page works at desktop and mobile widths.
- The three concepts are visibly drafts.
- Every visible button works or is removed.
- No payment, order, or customer data is collected.
- The demo clearly says it is not a live store.

OUT OF SCOPE TONIGHT
Live checkout, ecommerce accounts, print-on-demand integration, fulfillment and shipping policies, final brand identity, market validation, and launch claims.
